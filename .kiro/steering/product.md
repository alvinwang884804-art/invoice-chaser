# Product Steering — Invoice Chaser

## What it is

Invoice Chaser recovers overdue invoices for small trades businesses
(plumbers, HVAC, electricians, general contractors, 1–5 person shops).
The owner imports invoices once; the app chases payment automatically
so the owner can stay on the tools.

## Who it is for

- Solo tradespeople and tiny shops who invoice after the job and have no
  office staff to follow up.
- They are on their phones between jobs. The app must work well on mobile.
- They are not accountants. No jargon, no accounting workflows.

## Core value

One recovered invoice pays for a year of the product. The app earns its
keep the first time a reminder turns into a payment.

## Features (MVP)

1. **Dashboard** — outstanding total, overdue total, recovered this month,
   and the invoices that need attention first.
2. **Invoices** — add, import (CSV), and track invoices: client, amount,
   due date, status (sent / overdue / paid), days overdue.
3. **Clients** — the trades business's customers, with contact details
   for reminders.
4. **Reminder engine** — automatic email + SMS reminders at 1, 7, 14, and
   30 days overdue. Tone moves friendly → firm. Every reminder is logged.
5. **Templates** — editable reminder wording for each step and channel.
6. **Payments** — record payments (full or partial) and mark invoices paid;
   every invoice carries a payment link.
7. **Reports** — recovered amount over time, oldest unpaid, response rate.

## What it is NOT

- Not a full accounting system (no ledger, no tax, no payroll).
- Not a CRM. Client records exist only to send reminders and track payment.
- Not an invoicing tool — invoices come from the owner's existing system
  (import) or are entered in seconds.

## Tone

Plain, respectful, and firm. Reminders sound like a polite human, not a
collection agency. The tradesperson's reputation with their customer
matters more than any single invoice.
